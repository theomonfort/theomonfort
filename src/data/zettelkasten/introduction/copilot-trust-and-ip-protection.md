**Copilot Trust & IP Protection** brings together the legal, privacy, and compliance guarantees documented in the **GitHub Copilot Trust Center** for enterprise adopters of [[GitHub Copilot]].

## Enterprise Compliance Pillars

- **Public-Code Filtering & IP Protection**: Suggestions traversing [[Copilot Proxy]] can block verbatim matches against public GitHub code, unlocking **Microsoft Intellectual Property (IP) protection** for eligible commercial use.
- **Privacy & Non-Training Commitments**: Business and Enterprise customer prompts and suggestions are excluded from foundation model training by default.
- **Defense-in-Depth**: Combines legal and gateway safeguards with regional boundaries in [[Copilot Data Residency & FedRAMP]], policy controls in the [[Scale Pillar]], and automated vulnerability gates in the [[Trust Pillar]].
