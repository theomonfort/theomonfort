**Analyst Recognition & Market Leadership** summarizes enterprise adoption, independent analyst rankings, and measured developer productivity gains for [[GitHub]] and [[GitHub Copilot]].

## Market Adoption & Analyst Rankings (2026)

- **Scale**: **50M** total GitHub Copilot users (Microsoft FY26 Q4), **>4.7M** paid subscribers (FY26 Q2), and **~90% of the Fortune 100** adopting Copilot.
- **Gartner Magic Quadrant (2026)**: Named a **Leader for 3 consecutive years** in *Enterprise AI Coding Agents*, positioned **highest in Ability to Execute** across all 12 evaluated vendors (ahead of Cursor, Anthropic, OpenAI, Cognition, Google, AWS, Alibaba Cloud, Atlassian, Tabnine, BytePlus, and JetBrains).
- **IDC MarketScape**: Recognized as a **Leader** in *AI Coding and Software Engineering Technologies*.
