**Fusion Billing & Launch Pricing** governs how [[Copilot Fusion]] usage is metered across paid [[GitHub Copilot]] plans.

## Standard Metering & Effort Cost Mechanics

- **No Separate Fusion Fee**: Every step executed inside [[Fusion Execution Patterns]] bills at each underlying model's standard token rate, with zero extra orchestration surcharge.
- **Effort Drives Consumption**: Choosing `High` or `Max` in [[Fusion Effort Levels]] can invoke multiple models (`Cascade` or `Critique`) and consume more AI credits than `Standard`.

## Universe 2026 Launch Offer

- **30% Off Model Costs**: Users on **paid Copilot plans** receive a **30% discount on model token costs** for Copilot Fusion usage through **`2026-11-30`** (applies strictly to metered model costs, not base seat subscription pricing).
