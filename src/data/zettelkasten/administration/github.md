**GitHub** provides enterprise-grade hosting, identity lifecycle control, consolidated licensing, token-metered AI FinOps, layered governance, and cross-platform migration tooling.

## Enterprise Administration Architecture

- **Deployment & Data Residency**: Choose upfront between [[GitHub Enterprise Cloud]] on `github.com`, IdP-owned [[Enterprise Managed Users]], regional subdomain isolation on [[GHE.com Data Residency]], or self-hosted [[GitHub Enterprise Server]].
- **Identity & Provisioning**: Enforce centralized authentication and session revocation through [[SAML & OIDC SSO]] paired with automated IdP lifecycle provisioning.
- **Licensing & Seat Management**: Consolidate enterprise seats, multi-organization deduplication, and Copilot Business or Enterprise plans under [[GHE & Copilot Licenses]].
- **Usage-Based Billing & FinOps**: Meter AI tokens and cloud compute under [[Usage-Based Billing]], allocate spend across [[Cost Centers]], and enforce ceilings with [[Budgets & Spending Limits]].
- **Governance, Rulesets & AI Controls**: Cascade top-down access and policy controls via [[Enterprise Governance]], protect branches and tags with [[Enterprise & Repo Rulesets]], and lock down developer clients through [[Copilot Managed Settings]].
- **Enterprise Migrations**: Move organizations and repositories across hosting models and identity boundaries using [[GitHub Migrations]].
