**GitHub** is the central platform where team planning, repository context, and Copilot customizations live side by side.

## Planning & Copilot Primitives on GitHub

- **Planning & Tracking**: Open team Q&A in [[GitHub Discussions]], task decomposition and `Assign to Copilot` in [[Issues & Sub-issues]], and Table/Board/Roadmap tracking in [[GitHub Projects]].
- **Copilot Customizations**: Standing rules in [[Custom Instructions]], task-triggered workflows in [[Agent Skills]], portable bundles in [[Agent Plugins]], specialist personas in [[Custom Agents]], and lifecycle scripts in [[Hooks]].
- **Tools & Curated Context**: Live integrations via [[MCP Servers]], [[GitHub MCP Server]], and [[Copilot Connectors]], paired with [[Copilot Spaces]], [[Copilot Memory]], and [[Copilot Dreaming]].
- **Enterprise Governance**: Centralized client policies, sandboxing, and org/enterprise agent distribution via [[Copilot Managed Settings]].
