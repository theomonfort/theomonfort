The **Innovate Pillar** (`01 / Capability`) is the first of [[GitHub]]'s three Universe 2026 strategic pillars, focused on making [[GitHub Copilot]] useful from initial idea to shipped software across the [[AI Developer Platform]].

## Universe 2026 Availability Map (Checked 2026-11-11)

| Release | Status | Capability Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Copilot App** | `Available` | Goal-oriented desktop app; Projects and Computer Use in Public Preview ([[Agent HQ & Agent Teams]], [[Copilot Surfaces]]) |
| **Copilot Fusion** | `GA` | Multi-model routing and effort-based orchestration across IDEs, CLI, and App ([[Copilot Fusion]]) |
| **Copilot Automations** | `Available` | Shared, reusable team agent workflows rolling out in the app ([[Copilot Automations]]) |
| **Code Review Max** | `Preview` | Deeper agentic PR review; **Copilot Approvals** are `GA` ([[SDLC Stage 03 Review]]) |
| **Memory / Dreaming** | `Mixed` | **Copilot Memory** is `GA`; **Dreaming** is in `Public Preview` ([[SDLC Stage 05 Operate]]) |

Paired with the [[Scale Pillar]] and [[Trust Pillar]], Innovate turns isolated AI prompts into continuous multi-agent collaboration.
