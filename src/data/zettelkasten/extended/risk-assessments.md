**Risk Assessments** let organizations quantify real exposure on [[GitHub]] **for free before purchasing [[GHAS]]** (accessible from Organization > Security > Assessments).

## Two Free Pre-Purchase Scans

1. **Secret Risk Assessment**: Scans repositories across the organization to report leaked [[Provider & Generic Patterns]] counts and exposure categories before enabling full [[Secret Protection]] alert details and [[Push Protection]].
2. **Code Security Risk Assessment**: Runs [[CodeQL]] and [[Dependabot]] analysis across up to **20 active repositories** to surface concrete [[Code Security]] vulnerability counts and severities prior to full rollout via [[Security Configurations]].
