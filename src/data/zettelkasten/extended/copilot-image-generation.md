**Copilot Image Generation** (Public preview in the [[GitHub Copilot App]]) creates and iteratively edits PNG assets, icons, and visual mocks directly inside chat.

## Eligibility & Governance

- **Plans & Billing**: Available on paid Copilot plans (excludes Copilot Free and Student) and consumes separately billed AI credits.
- **Policy Gate**: Requires the administrator's image model policy to allow image generation.
- **Deliverable Workflow**: Generated visuals can be refined across turns and embedded into [[Native Office Canvases]] or stored inside [[Copilot App Projects]] Libraries.
