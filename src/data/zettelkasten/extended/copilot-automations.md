**Copilot Automations** (`Available`, announced at Universe `2026-10-28` under the [[Innovate Pillar]]) let teams package and share recurring agentic workflows inside [[GitHub Copilot]] and the Copilot App.

## Key Capabilities

- **Shared Team Workflows**: Convert repeatable triage, reporting, review check-ins, and cross-repo maintenance prompts into durable automations usable across a team's [[Copilot Surfaces]].
- **Project & Session Integration**: Coordinates cleanly with persistent Projects in [[Agent HQ & Agent Teams]], waking dedicated or child sessions on schedules or triggers.
- **Repository Operations Bridge**: Complements GitHub Actions-hosted Agentic Workflows in [[SDLC Stage 05 Operate]] and the [[Scale Pillar]].

## Connected Automation Primitives

- Complements repository-scoped [[Agentic Workflows]] on [[GitHub Actions]] and multi-repo orchestration in [[GitHub Copilot App]] and [[Copilot App Projects]].
