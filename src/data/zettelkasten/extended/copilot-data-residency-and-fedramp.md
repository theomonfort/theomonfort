**Copilot Data Residency & FedRAMP** (shipped `2026-04-13`) extends regional sovereign hosting and government compliance guarantees directly into [[GitHub Copilot]] model routing.

## Regional & Regulated Model Controls

- **US & EU Data Residency**: Keeps Copilot prompt processing and model inference strictly inside **US** or **EU** geographic boundaries when paired with regional enterprise deployments.
- **FedRAMP-Authorized Models**: Restricts public-sector and regulated workloads to **FedRAMP-authorized** model endpoints.
- **Policy-Aware Routing Integration**: Regional and FedRAMP constraints automatically filter the eligible model pool enforced by [[Copilot Proxy]], [[Copilot Multi-Model Catalog & BYOK]], and [[Copilot Fusion]], reinforcing [[Copilot Trust & IP Protection]].
