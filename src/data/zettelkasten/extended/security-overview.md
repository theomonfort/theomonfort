**Security Overview** is the organization- and enterprise-level command center for [[GHAS]] on [[GitHub]], aggregating posture across [[Secret Protection]], [[Code Security]], [[Code Scanning]], and [[Dependabot]].

## Five Executive & AppSec Views

- **Overview & Risk**: Ranks repositories and teams by open critical/high vulnerabilities, EPSS exploitability, [[Reachability Analysis]], and active leaked credentials.
- **Coverage**: Shows exact enablement percentages for [[Push Protection]], [[CodeQL]], and Dependabot, making gaps easy to close with [[Security Configurations]].
- **Remediation & Campaigns**: Tracks MTTR (mean time to remediate), net-new vs closed trends, and [[Security Campaigns]] burn-down.
- **[[Public Monitoring]]**: Dedicated enterprise view for credentials leaked in public repositories outside your orgs.
