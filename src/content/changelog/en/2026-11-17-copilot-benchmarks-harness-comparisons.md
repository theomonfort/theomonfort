### Compare harnesses

The new **Harnesses** view compares GitHub Copilot CLI, Claude Code, and Codex CLI using the same model at the same reasoning effort. A scatter plot shows resolution rate against mean cost per task, with ellipses representing variation across published runs.

Paired results show Copilot CLI's resolution difference in percentage points and cost savings as a percentage, with approximate 95% bootstrap intervals. Only exact model pairs at medium reasoning effort are included. Copilot-only configurations such as Copilot Fusion and unmatched results are excluded.

### Explore scientific workflows

**Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1** evaluates agents on research workflows across scientific domains, extending the site beyond software engineering benchmarks. Its tasks are authored and reviewed by domain experts.

Both additions are in public preview. Explore them on the [GitHub Copilot benchmarks site](https://benchmarks.github.com/?utm_source=benchmark-changelog-benchmark-site-2&utm_medium=changelog&utm_campaign=ignite-nov-2026).
