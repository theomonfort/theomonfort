### What's changing

- **New default**: Starting December 30, reviews by members with a Copilot license will be billed to the organization that owns the repository if no billing choice is saved. Existing **Member** or **Organization** choices stay in effect.
- **Keep member billing**: Select **Member** in the enterprise or organization Copilot policy settings before the change. Accounts without AI Credits paid usage, with a billing lock, or without a billable customer will default to **Member** instead.
- **Scope and cost**: This change applies to organizations and enterprises with Copilot Business or Enterprise. Organization billing requires AI Credits paid usage and may increase organization spending. The separate setting for members without a Copilot license is unchanged.
