### Work with GHES in the Copilot app

GHES support is now generally available, following an opt-in preview. Add your GHES account to access its remote repositories, issues, and pull requests from the Copilot app.

1. Sign in to the app with a GitHub.com or GHE.com account.
2. Go to **Settings → Accounts → Add account** and choose **GitHub Enterprise Server**.
3. Follow the prompts to authorize the app.

Available in Copilot app **1.2.0 and later**. To make requests, also sign in with a cloud account that has a Copilot license or configure your own model provider. Cloud sessions are not available for GHES.
