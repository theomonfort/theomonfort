### Key takeaways

- **Less filter syntax to memorize.** Content assist helps build filters. Combine conditions with `AND` / `OR` and nested searches.
- **Jump straight to your PRs.** The collapsible sidebar includes shortcuts such as Authored by me and Involves me.
- **More context without opening each PR.** See status checks, stack indicators, unread updates, milestones, and linked issues. Compact view fits more PRs on screen.
- **New since preview**: bulk actions for closing, labeling, and changing milestones. Click a review status to filter the list.
