### Work across repositories and sessions

- **Projects**: describe a goal, attach repositories, designs, conversations, or notes, and choose models and tools. Copilot coordinates agents and returns results such as pull requests for review.
- **Project automations**: repeat tasks when needed, rather than rebuilding the context every time.
- **Work beyond code**: use connected tools such as Slack and Figma to research a request, draft a specification, or iterate on designs. Generate and edit images in chat.

Focused, single-repository sessions remain available. Projects are in public preview.

### Work with desktop apps

Computer use is in public preview on **macOS and Windows**. Copilot can read app content, click controls, and enter or edit text, including in apps without an API or MCP integration.

Enable it in **Settings → Computer Use**. Copilot asks for approval before controlling an app; organizations can disable the feature through managed settings. On macOS, Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions are required.
