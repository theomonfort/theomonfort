### Key takeaways

- **Multiple repositories, one conversation**: create issues, make code changes and open PRs, track your open PRs, or investigate bugs across frontend and backend repositories without starting a new conversation.
- **More natural interactions**: mention @GitHub anywhere in a message, not just at the beginning, and switch repositories more smoothly.
- **Better context and reliability**: shared Slack messages and threads can provide context. Task startup, error handling, and recovery from interruptions are improved in both Slack and Teams.

### Previous update

The [September 25 update](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-25-updates-to-github-copilot-for-slack-and-microsoft-teams/) added richer context from Slack files and message links, plus Teams images, forwarded messages, and conversation history. It also introduced model switching that persists for the conversation, similar-issue checks before issue creation, and links back to the original discussion.
