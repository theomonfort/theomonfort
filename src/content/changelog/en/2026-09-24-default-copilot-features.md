### Key takeaways

- **Configure now**: AI Controls → Copilot → Default policy for new features. Choose Enabled, Disabled, or Let organizations decide.
- Covers current and future eligible GA features, including **Copilot code review and MCP servers**.
- **Explicit feature settings are preserved. Preview features remain opt-in.**
