### Key takeaways

- **Less filter syntax to memorize.** Content assist helps build filters. Combine conditions with `AND` / `OR` and nested searches.
- **Jump straight to your PRs.** The collapsible sidebar includes shortcuts such as Authored by me and Involves me.
- **More context without opening each PR.** See status check counts, stack indicators, and unread updates. Compact view fits more PRs on screen.

### Before you switch

The preview is available to all GitHub users. Milestone display, bulk updates, and saved custom views are not yet supported, and label emojis may not appear. To return to the classic page, select **Preview → Switch back** at the top.
