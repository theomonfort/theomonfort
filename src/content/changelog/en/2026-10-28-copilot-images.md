### From a request to a project asset

- **Natural-language creation and editing**: ask for a visual during a coding task without moving to another tool.
- **Inline preview and local output**: Copilot displays the result in chat and saves it to the workspace.
- **Dedicated image tool**: your reasoning model calls GPT Image 2.5 Flare for image generation rather than generating the image itself.
- **Optional watermark**: “Made with AI” watermarking is available and off by default.

### Availability and controls

Public preview in **VS Code, Copilot app, and CLI** for Copilot Pro, Pro+, Business, and Enterprise.

Image generation is **billed separately from the chat model**, through image tokens charged against AI credits. Organization and enterprise owners can disable the image-generation model through model policies.
